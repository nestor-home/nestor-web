Nestor is an open-source assistant for the whole household. A Python server at home runs one agent,
on a language model, hosted or local. Everyone reaches it their own way: aloud through a satellite
in the kitchen, in the admin's chat, on Telegram when away. The project is young: expect changes
between releases.

## What it does

<Cards cols={3}>
  <Card title="Knows who asks" href="/docs/agent/speakers/" icon="mic">Keeps each person's conversations and memories apart.</Card>
  <Card title="Remembers" href="/docs/agent/memory/" icon="brain">Recalls what you told it, with dates.</Card>
  <Card title="Runs the house" href="/docs/home/" brand="home-assistant">Through Home Assistant, and knows who is home.</Card>
  <Card title="Keeps watch" href="/docs/agent/initiative/" icon="sparkles">Tells you when the mail you wait for arrives.</Card>
  <Card title="Finds the right person" href="/docs/agent/initiative/" icon="chat">In their room or on their phone, quiet at night.</Card>
  <Card title="Keeps a journal" href="/docs/agent/journal/" icon="journal">Every action and its reason, shown in the admin.</Card>
</Cards>

## Your first hour

<Steps>

1. **Install the server** with uv, pip or Docker: [Installation](/docs/install/).
2. **Have a first conversation** in the admin and in a terminal: [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart/).
3. **Add a channel** such as Telegram, to reach it from anywhere: [Channels](/docs/channels/).
4. **Add a satellite** in a room, to talk to it aloud: [Satellites](/docs/satellites/).

</Steps>
