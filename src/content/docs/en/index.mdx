Nestor is an open-source assistant for the whole household: one agent on a server at home, reached
aloud through satellites, in the admin's chat or on Telegram. Nestor is a preview: a release may change the configuration or the
journal without a migration.

<AdminShot name="today" locale="en" alt="The admin's Today page: what Nestor is doing, the rooms and who is in them, what waits for you" />

## What it does

<Cards cols={3}>
  <Card title="Knows who asks" href="/docs/agent/speakers/" icon="mic">Each person's conversations and memories apart.</Card>
  <Card title="Remembers" href="/docs/agent/memory/" icon="brain">What you told it, with dates.</Card>
  <Card title="Runs the house" href="/docs/home/" brand="home-assistant">Through Home Assistant.</Card>
  <Card title="Keeps watch" href="/docs/agent/initiative/" icon="sparkles">Tells you when the awaited mail arrives.</Card>
  <Card title="Finds the right person" href="/docs/agent/initiative/" icon="chat">In their room or on their phone.</Card>
  <Card title="Keeps a journal" href="/docs/agent/journal/" icon="journal">Every action and its reason.</Card>
</Cards>

## Your first hour

<Steps>
