One agent answers the whole household, aloud or in writing, with one memory.

## The parts

<SystemMap locale="en" n={1} />

The **core** depends on no vendor: models, Home Assistant, connectors, executors and channels are
**adapters** behind contracts. The [journal](/docs/agent/journal/) records every effect and
decision; a restart rebuilds open conversations, tasks, reminders and undelivered messages from it.

## A turn

<TurnFlow locale="en" n={2} />

A **conversation** groups turns close in time, as in Home Assistant's Assist. Once it ends, the
model no longer rereads its turns; memories carry over. It has one turn at a time: messages sent
together are answered in order.

Aloud, Nestor speaks while the model is still writing. An acknowledgement ("ok", "mm") does not
stop it; any other word does, as does 1.2 seconds of speech, and the journal keeps what was heard.
The acknowledgements it knows are French ones, with "ok". When the model or a voice service fails
or stalls, Nestor says so (`conversation.fallback`).

## Tools

Short actions are direct tools: a light, a memory, a reminder. A tool slower than the turn's wait
goes on in the background; its result comes back to the conversation later and Nestor tells the
person. A long result is stored aside: the model sees its start, end and size, and reads the rest
with `read_result` and `search_result`. Work that takes minutes goes to an
[executor](/docs/executors/).

| Setting                                  | Default                     | What it does                            |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `agent.spoken_tool_wait_s`               | 3                           | seconds a spoken turn waits for a tool  |
| `agent.written_tool_wait_s`              | 20                          | seconds a written turn waits for a tool |
| `agent.tool_result_chars`                | 8000                        | longer results are stored aside         |
| `conversation_ends_after_s`, per channel | 5 min aloud, 4 h in writing | silence that ends a conversation        |

## The instructions

<PromptLayers locale="en" n={3} />

The household layer also says how the agent runs right now, such as a web search on free tiers;
the agent mentions it when it bears on the request. A plugin reports it through its component's
`notes()`, in English.

## Models by role

Name model profiles in `models` and point roles at them. A single model needs nothing more.

```yaml
models:
  quick:
    type: language
    provider: openai-compatible
    base_url: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1
    api_key: ${MODEL_API_KEY}
    model: anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5
  careful:
    type: language
    provider: openai-compatible
    base_url: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1
    api_key: ${MODEL_API_KEY}
    model: anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5

agent:
  spoken: quick
  written: careful
```

| Role                   | Does                                     | When left out   |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| `agent.spoken`         | answers aloud                            | `agent.written` |
| `agent.written`        | answers in writing                       | `agent.spoken`  |
| `agent.titles`         | titles each chat                         | `agent.written` |
| `watches.model`        | sorts incoming items against the watches | `agent.written` |
| `memory.consolidation` | summarises each finished conversation    | `agent.written` |

Voice roles are on [Voice](/docs/voice/), every role on [Models](/docs/models/).
