Nestor runs one agent for the household. Speak to it in the kitchen, write to it from the office:
it is the same agent, with the same memory. This page shows its parts and follows a message from
the moment it arrives to the answer. [Memory](/docs/agent/memory/) and
[Initiative](/docs/agent/initiative/) go further into what it remembers and when it speaks first.

## The parts

<SystemMap locale="en" n={1} />

The **core** holds the conversation agent and the modules around it: memory, initiative, delegated
tasks, the household. It depends on no vendor. Everything outside it is an **adapter** behind a
contract: the models, Home Assistant, connectors, executors, and the channels people use.

The **journal** is the source of truth. Each effect is an event appended to it: a turn, a tool call,
a memory, a delivery, a policy decision with its reason. The admin shows it as conversation threads,
and a restart rebuilds the running state from it: open conversations, tasks to follow, wake-ups to
come, messages not delivered yet.

## A turn

<TurnFlow locale="en" n={2} />

A **conversation** groups turns close in time, as Home Assistant's Assist does. After five minutes
of silence aloud, or four hours in writing, the next turn opens a new one and the model no longer
rereads the earlier turns. Each channel can set its own window with `conversation_ends_after_s`.
Memories cross every conversation.

The agent stays fast because nothing long happens inside a turn. What is frequent and short is a
direct tool (turn on a light, remember a fact, set a wake-up). What takes minutes goes to an
**executor** as a durable task: the agent says it has started, the task runs in the background, and
the result is announced once, to whoever asked.

## The instructions

<PromptLayers locale="en" n={3} />

The instructions are built for each turn, in layers ordered from the most stable to the most
variable. A provider that caches prompts reads everything before a cache point from its cache,
faster and at a fraction of the price. A
new turn in the same household pays in full only for its turn layer and the new messages.

Each module adds its own sections to a layer: memory adds what is remembered to the household layer,
and the past conversations of whoever speaks to the turn layer; initiative adds the watches and the
wake-ups; messaging adds the questions still waiting for an answer.

## Models by role

The models are profiles you name in `models`, and each role points to one of them. With a single
model, there is nothing else to write.

```yaml
models:
  quick:
    kind: openai-compatible
    base_url: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1
    api_key: ${MODEL_API_KEY}
    model: anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5
  careful:
    kind: openai-compatible
    base_url: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1
    api_key: ${MODEL_API_KEY}
    model: anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5

agent:
  spoken: quick      # the voice: the first words come fast
  written: careful   # writing allows a few seconds
  background: quick  # wake-ups; the written model by default
watches:
  model: quick       # sorts incoming mail against the watches
memory:
  consolidation: quick  # summarizes each conversation once it is over
```

A role left out takes another one's model: `spoken` and `written` stand in for each other,
`background` and the watches take `written`, and the consolidation takes `background`.
