Speak to Nestor in the kitchen or write from the office: one agent answers, with one memory. Here
are its parts, and a message followed to its answer.

## The parts

<SystemMap locale="en" n={1} />

The **core** depends on no vendor. Models, Home Assistant, connectors, executors and channels are
**adapters** behind contracts.

The **journal** is the source of truth. Every effect is an event in it, down to each policy decision
and its reason. The admin shows it as conversation threads. A restart rebuilds from it the open
conversations, tasks, wake-ups and undelivered messages.

## A turn

<TurnFlow locale="en" n={2} />

A **conversation** groups turns close in time, as in Home Assistant's Assist. After five minutes of
silence aloud, or four hours in writing, a new one starts and the model stops rereading the old
turns. Each channel can change this with `conversation_ends_after_s`. Memories cross conversations.

Nothing long happens inside a turn. Short actions are direct tools: turn on a light, remember a
fact, set a wake-up. Work that takes minutes goes to an **executor** as a durable task, and its
result is announced once, to whoever asked.

## The instructions

<PromptLayers locale="en" n={3} />

Each turn builds its instructions in layers, from the most stable to the most variable. A provider
that caches prompts reads everything before a cache point from its cache, faster and cheaper. A new
turn pays in full only for its turn layer and the new messages.

Each module fills its own sections: memory, initiative (watches, wake-ups), messaging (open
questions).

## Models by role

Name model profiles in `models` and point each role to one. A single model needs nothing more.

```yaml
models:
  quick:
    kind: openai-compatible
    base_url: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1
    api_key: ${MODEL_API_KEY}
    model: anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5
  careful:
    kind: openai-compatible
    base_url: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1
    api_key: ${MODEL_API_KEY}
    model: anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5

agent:
  spoken: quick      # the voice: the first words come fast
  written: careful   # writing allows a few seconds
  background: quick  # wake-ups; the written model by default
watches:
  model: quick       # sorts incoming mail against the watches
memory:
  consolidation: quick  # summarizes each conversation once it is over
```

A role left out borrows a model: `spoken` and `written` stand in for each other, `background` and
the watches take `written`, the consolidation takes `background`.
