Nestor remembers two things: **memories**, lasting facts it was told ("Léa is vegetarian"), and
**episodes**, what each past conversation was about. Both are rebuilt from the journal, which keeps
every word. Memory is generous: a turn carries more than it strictly needs, because a line the
model skips costs little and a memory it lacks costs a wrong answer.

## What a turn carries

<MemoryTurn locale="en" n={4} />

The **household layer** carries the latest memories, up to 150, each dated so that of two memories
that disagree the model keeps the later one. They are the same from one turn to the next, so the
provider's cache keeps them. When a household has more, the **turn layer** adds those the last three
messages of whoever speaks find among the others.

The turn layer also carries the **episodes** of whoever speaks: their three latest conversations,
then older ones their words find. An episode is seen only by the people who took part in it; what
concerns the whole household goes to the memories.

The agent reaches the rest with tools. `recall` searches memories and conversations, between two
dates when asked ("what did we say last week?"). `read_conversation` rereads one conversation from
the journal, word for word, for the people who took part in it.

## Finding by words and by meaning

<HybridSearch locale="en" n={5} />

Every search runs twice. **By words**, SQLite's full-text index ranks what shares words with the
question, accents ignored. **By meaning**, an embedding model turns the question into a vector, and
the nearest vectors come out of [sqlite-vec](https://github.com/asg017/sqlite-vec). Reciprocal rank
fusion merges both lists: what is high in either comes high, what is in both comes first.

The meaning half is optional. Name an embedding model in `models` and point `memory.embedder` at it:

```yaml
models:
  embed:
    kind: openai-compatible-embeddings
    base_url: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1
    api_key: ${MODEL_API_KEY}
    model: openai/text-embedding-3-small
memory:
  embedder: embed
```

Any server that speaks the OpenAI embeddings API works, a local one included (llama.cpp's
`llama-server --embeddings`). Vectors are computed in the background as memories are written: a turn
never waits for them. When a question cannot be embedded within a second (`memory.query_timeout_s`),
that turn searches by words only. Changing the model reindexes everything in the background.

## When a conversation is over

<Consolidation locale="en" n={6} />

A conversation is over once its silence is longer than any medium of its place allows. A small
model then reads it and writes its **episode**: two or three sentences and a few topics, for the
people who took part. It also writes down the lasting facts the agent did not remember during the
conversation. Both are journaled. If Nestor was stopped in the meantime, it catches up at the next
start, never twice.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `memory.recent` | 150 | memories in the household layer |
| `memory.found` | 12 | memories the words may add beyond them |
| `memory.recent_episodes` | 3 | latest conversations of whoever speaks |
| `memory.found_episodes` | 4 | older conversations their words may add |
| `memory.embedder` | none | the embedding model, for search by meaning |
| `memory.consolidation` | `agent.background` | the model that writes episodes |
