Most of the time Nestor answers. Sometimes it speaks first: a reminder someone asked for, a mail
it was told to watch for, a task that finished, a question to pass on to someone else. All of them
take the same road.

<Initiative locale="en" n={7} />

## What calls for it

**Wake-ups.** "Remind me tomorrow at 9 to call the plumber", "every weekday at 8, tell me the
weather": the agent calls `schedule` with an instruction complete enough to act on alone, and a time
once or times on chosen days, in the household's clock. When it is due, the agent wakes up for a
background turn with its tools, on the `agent.background` model. A wake-up missed while Nestor was
stopped runs once at the next start.

**Watches.** "Tell me when the bank writes": a watch is created from the conversation. Each incoming
mail is read once by a small model against every watch at the same time, and a match is announced
to whoever asked, urgent or not.

**Tasks.** When an executor finishes a task, its result is announced once to whoever asked, even
after a restart.

**Messages.** "Tell Hugo dinner is at eight", "ask Léa if she needs the car": the agent calls
`message_person`. A message that expects a reply opens a question, which both of them see in their
turns until the answer comes back and the agent passes it on. Each person's `may_message` says whom
they may have the agent write to.

## Where and how it arrives

The **courier** decides first, and writes its decision and the reason to the journal:

- the person asked for writing, or is away: a message where they read (Telegram today);
- they are home: spoken by the satellite of the room they are in, or by the household's main
  satellite when the home only knows they are home;
- quiet hours: nothing spoken unless urgent, and the message still goes in writing;
- no presence at all: the conversation the request came from, else a message.

The agent then words the message for that place and medium, knowing what was said there in the
open conversation. When nobody reacts to a spoken message, the courier follows up in writing.

Presence comes from Home Assistant, at the granularity the home has: a room when it knows, only home
or away when that is all it knows.
