Nestor sometimes speaks first: a reminder you asked for, a mail it watched for, a finished task, a
question from someone else. All of them take the same road.

<Initiative locale="en" n={7} />

## What calls for it

**Wake-ups.** "Remind me tomorrow at 9 to call the plumber", "every weekday at 8, tell me the
weather". The agent calls `schedule` with a self-contained instruction and a time, once or on chosen
days. When due, it runs a background turn on the `agent.background` model. A wake-up missed while
Nestor was stopped runs once at the next start.

**Watches.** "Tell me when the bank writes" creates a watch. A small model reads each incoming mail
once against all watches and announces a match to whoever asked.

**Tasks.** An executor's result is announced once to whoever asked, even after a restart.

**Messages.** "Tell Hugo dinner is at eight", "ask Léa if she needs the car": the agent calls
`message_person`. A message that expects a reply opens a question, which both see until the answer
comes back. Each person's `may_message` lists whom they may write to through the agent.

## Where and how it arrives

The **courier** decides first and journals its decision with the reason:

- the person asked for writing, or is away: a message where they read (Telegram today);
- they are home: spoken in their room, or by the main satellite when their room is unknown;
- quiet hours: nothing spoken unless urgent, and the message still goes in writing;
- no presence at all: a message; said aloud only back into a spoken conversation still going on,
  or to someone with nowhere to write;
- something private (a mail for someone): the room only hears that something came, and the content
  is written at once.

Aloud means in front of the room: anyone there hears it. See [Who is speaking](/docs/agent/speakers/).

Once delivered, what Nestor brought (the task's result, the mail) and its message become a turn of that
conversation, as a tool's result does in a turn: answer it, and the agent knows what it was about.

Presence comes from Home Assistant: a room when it knows, home or away otherwise.
